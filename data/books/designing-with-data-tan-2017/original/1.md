# Foreword
This book offers a simple but exciting promise for anyone involved with the design of internet products:


If you compare what you think should happen (in theory) to what actually happens (in reality), you can better understand how your work changes user behavior.


This book is your training camp to getting started using data — design’s often overlooked but essential companion — to improve your work. You can augment this approach with your existing design practices to amplify the impact of each design: from identifying the problem you are solving, to designing for experimentation, to learning from the data.


What’s unique about this training camp are the field guides at hand: not only your authors sharing experiences from the world’s leading data-driven organizations, but also the contributions from many practitioners offering you the advantage of their standard practices, case studies, and hard-fought lessons from designing with data every single day.


COLIN MCFARLAND,


HEAD OF EXPERIMENTATION AT SKYSCANNER
