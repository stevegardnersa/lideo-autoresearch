# Inventing Our Best Lives
Depending on how you look, you can glimpse two futures for our lives with technology. Put on your rose-colored virtual-reality headset, and you’ll see the promise of unparalleled ease. Scroll, bleary-eyed, through your social media feed and it’s a cacophony of voices foretelling the collapse of the world as we know it. What can these future visions tell us about our emotional relationship with technology?
