# Top Thirty AI Books
In the Notes section, I provide citations of the books and articles that are discussed or referred to in the body of the text. Rather than repeat them all here, I instead lay out below my top thirty favourite AI books. None is technical. They focus largely on the impact of AI, which is the heart of this book too. I find these readings are, variously, informative, insightful, inspiring, impactful, and mind-boggling.
