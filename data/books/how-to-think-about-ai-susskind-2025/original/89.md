# Acknowledgements
This book is dedicated to the memory of my wonderful dad. With paternal indulgence and much love, he read all my books diligently. I hope he would have liked this one. He preferred my work when I had mustered the confidence to write simply, which I have tried to do in these pages.


My relationship with Oxford University Press is almost forty years of age. This is my eighth OUP book, although we have worked together on sixteen different volumes (taking account of all the various English versions and editions). It is a tribute to OUP that they have put up with me so affably on all sixteen occasions. I am aware that I am annoying—always late in submitting the manuscript and, immediately thereafter, unreasonably demanding on time scales for marketing and publication. I also agitate over design, typeface, paper opacity, jacket design, legalities, and more. I can only apologize. This time round, my sincere thanks go to Dominic Byatt, Luciana O’Flaherty, Tara Werger, Anna Silva, and Emma Slaughter.


I am unusually privileged to have two AI-related experts in my own clan—my sons, Daniel and Jamie. Their own thinking and writing pervades this book, and their support, encouragement, and love are deeply appreciated; as of course were their incisive comments on various drafts.


Still in the family, my incomparable brother Alan was unfailingly supportive of this project, as he has been through my whole life.


Thank you to Tom Melham for reading the entire manuscript and helping me purge the text of technological errors.


I took great comfort from the encouraging reactions to the final draft from David Gold and Conrad Young, who also turn out to be world-class proof readers.


If I know anything about the world of start-ups, it is from my beloved daughter, Ali. At the same time, she is perhaps the wisest of all of us, and I regularly seek and follow her counsel. I am the luckiest of fathers.


Finally, my thanks to my wife, Michelle, who draws deeply on her talents as a cognitive behavioural therapist in helping me, each time, through the tortuous process of finishing a book. Her unswerving confidence in my work inspires me much more than she realises—I am profoundly grateful.


Richard Susskind,Radlett, England,18 December 2024
