## Footnotes
Founded by Paul Graham, Y Combinator put start-up accelerator programs on the map, providing a ten-week bootcamp that put founding teams through a rigorous process to help them get their ideas in shape and launch something. Paul vetted the applicants to the program and gave the founders he selected a stipend so they could support themselves as they tested their ideas to see if they might turn into real companies.


DARPA is the Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency, the research and development arm of the US Department of Defense.
