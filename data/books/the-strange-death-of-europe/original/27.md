A NOTE ON THE AUTHOR


Douglas Murray is Associate Editor of the Spectator and writes frequently for a variety of other publications, including the Sunday Times and Wall Street Journal. An experienced debater, Douglas has spoken on a variety of prominent platforms, including at the British and European Parliaments and at the White House.


His previous books include Bloody Sunday: Truth, Lies and the Saville Inquiry (joint winner of the 2011-12 Christopher Ewart-Biggs Memorial Prize for advancing peace and understanding), Neoconservatism: Why We Need It and Bosie: A Biography of Lord Alfred Douglas.
